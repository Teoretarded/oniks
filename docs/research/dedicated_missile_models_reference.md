# Dedicated Missile Model Reference

Research date: 2026-06-13. Scope: all in-game missile models except P-800
Oniks, which already has a reference-built mesh.

## BGM-109 Tomahawk

- Shape used: 6.25 m cruise body, 0.527 m diameter, straight pop-out mid wings,
  cruciform tail fins, ventral turbofan intake and dark tail nozzle.
- Primary references: U.S. Navy Tomahawk overview and public photo pages:
  `https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169229/tomahawk-cruise-missile/`;
  dimensions/photo cross-check: `https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/tomahawk/`,
  `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BGM-109_Tomahawk_Ship-launched_Cruise_Missile.jpg`.

## AGM-158 JASSM

- Shape used: 4.27 m compact faceted stealth body, trapezoid pop-out wings,
  flush dorsal intake, clipped tail controls.
- Primary references: U.S. Air Force art reference:
  `https://www.af.mil/News/Art/igphoto/2000420243/`; dimensions/photo
  cross-check: `https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/jassm/`.

## AGM-88 HARM

- Shape used: 4.17 m x 0.254 m slim tube, tan seeker nose, large cruciform
  mid-body wings, smaller cruciform tail fins, warning bands.
- Primary references: U.S. Air Force fact sheet and photo:
  `https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104574/agm-88-harm/`.

## AIM-9X Sidewinder

- Shape used: 3.0 m x 0.127 m very slim body, glass seeker, small forward
  double-delta canards and rear rolleron fins.
- Primary references: U.S. Navy AIM-9X fact file/photo:
  `https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2168989/aim-9x-sidewinder-missile/`;
  U.S. Air Force Sidewinder dimensions:
  `https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104557/aim-9-sidewinder/`.

## 48N6

- Shape used: existing 7.5 m S-300 48N6 reference model: clean light-grey
  cylinder, ogive/dark nose, clipped tail controls and low-profile strakes.
- Primary references: `docs/research/s300_reference.md`; 48N6 dimensions:
  `https://weaponsystems.net/system/1417-%3E%3E48N6`; local S-300/48N6
  reference images in `docs/research/img/`.

## 40N6

- Shape used: 8.0 m 48N6-family long-range round, same 0.515 m diameter,
  cleaner mid-body, active-seeker nose band, slightly larger tail controls.
- Primary references: public 40N6E/S-400 dimension references:
  `https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/russia/s-400-missiles.htm`,
  `https://www.ausairpower.net/APA-S-400-Triumf.html`; 48N6-family exposed
  round photos where 40N6 exposed photos are not public enough to rely on.

## SM-2

- Shape used: 6.55 m game round, 0.343 m diameter, Standard Missile cylinder,
  long pointed nose, tail controls, darker booster/nozzle section and bands.
- Primary references: U.S. Navy Standard Missile fact file:
  `https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169011/standard-missile/`.

## Pantsir 57E6

- Shape used: 3.17 m two-stage bicaliber missile: fat rear booster, thin
  forward dart, small tail-control fins, no broad mid-body wings.
- Primary references: CSIS Pantsir S-1:
  `https://missilethreat.csis.org/defsys/pantsir-s-1/`; public 57E6/Pantsir-S1
  display photos.

## 3M22 Zircon / Tsirkon Prototype

- Shape used: 9.0 m, ~0.6 m class speculative hypersonic cruise missile:
  dark chined lifting body, flattened sides, ventral scramjet inlet/duct,
  small clipped fins and dark rear exhaust.
- Important uncertainty: public imagery of Zircon is limited and often uses
  illustrations. The model intentionally reads as a plausible scramjet
  hypersonic missile, not a guaranteed production-airframe copy.
- Primary references: RUSI overview:
  `https://www.rusi.org/explore-our-research/publications/commentary/zircon-how-much-threat-does-russias-hypersonic-missile-pose`;
  Missile Defense Advocacy Alliance:
  `https://www.missiledefenseadvocacy.org/missile-threat-and-proliferation/todays-missile-threat/russia/3m22-zircon/`;
  ODIN/WEG:
  `https://odin.t2com.army.mil/WEG/Asset/3M22_Tsirkon_Russian_Medium-Range_Cruise_Missile`;
  Ukrainian debris/diagram coverage:
  `https://kyivindependent.com/hypersonic-zircon-missiles-russias-latest-threat/`.

## SM-6 / RIM-174 Standard ERAM Prototype

- Shape used: 6.55 m Standard Missile body, 0.343 m upper diameter, fat
  0.53 m Mk 72 booster aft, large booster steering fins, low upper-stage
  dorsal/cable fairings, dark AMRAAM-derived seeker nose and colored bands.
- Primary references: U.S. Navy Standard Missile fact file:
  `https://www.navy.mil/Resources/Fact-Files/Display-FactFiles/Article/2169011/standard-missile/`;
  CSIS SM-6:
  `https://missilethreat.csis.org/defsys/sm-6/`;
  Raytheon SM-6 product overview:
  `https://www.rtx.com/raytheon/what-we-do/sea/sm-6-missile`;
  Designation Systems RIM-174 dimensions:
  `https://www.designation-systems.net/dusrm/m-174.html`.
