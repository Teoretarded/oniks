> Steps use checkbox syntax. The LOCKED CONVENTIONS of

**Goal:** Add a second weapon system — an S-300-style long-range air-defense interceptor,
cold-launched from a new 4-tube 5P85-style TEL at a second friendly site — plus enemy patrol
aircraft to intercept, platform switching, and the visual-polish backlog (Bastion art pass,
terrain/ocean look). User mandate: quality-check, optimize, and visually judge everything;
iterate until genuinely good.

**Architecture:** New `SamDef`/`S300` in the data-driven arsenal; `sim/sam.py` SamMissile with
its own phase machine (catapult eject → vertical boost+tilt → midcourse predicted-intercept
steer → terminal PN with proximity fuse), duck-typed into `world.missiles`; `sim/aircraft.py`
racetrack patrol aircraft feeding the existing ContactBoard as air tracks; new models; TAB
platform switching in the sandbox; map gains air symbols + SAM range ring.

**Baseline:** v1 complete at `feat: ONIKS v1 sandbox complete` (143 tests green, perf harness
11.49 ms avg worst-case). Nothing here may regress that: full suite + perf gate re-run at the end.

## Locked numbers for this expansion

- **S300 weapon (48N6-style, simplified single-stage):** `weapon_id="s300"`, display
  "S-300 48N6", length 7.5 m, diameter 0.515 m, launch_mass 1900 kg, propellant 1020 kg,
  catapult eject 30 m/s vertical for 0.6 s, motor thrust 200 kN for 12 s (mdot = thrust/(240*9.81)),
  ref_area 0.208 m², `cd_from_mach` reused, max_g 25, proximity fuse radius 25 m,
  terminal PN range 20 km, max guided range vs air ≈ 150 km, self-destruct at t>180 s or
  when post-burnout speed < 250 m/s, intercept altitudes 0.1–25 km. Peak speed ~Mach 5.5–6.
- **S300 launcher:** `launcher_id="s300_tel"`, display "5P85 TEL", 4 tubes, reload 8 s between
  shots (tube-to-tube), ammo 4.
- **SAM_SITE** (friendly, NOT a target): on home-coast land far east of base, nominal
  `(85_000, terrain, -3_500)` — implementer must verify `terrain_height > 5` there and nudge z
  south (more negative) until on land, then freeze the constant in `world/generation.py` as
  `SAM_SITE_POS` with a test.
- **Aircraft:** types `patrol` (170 m/s, alt 6500 m, length 30 m, wingspan 35 m) and
  `fast` (240 m/s, alt 4000 m, length 20 m, wingspan 14 m). 4 spawns, racetrack legs 60–100 km,
  turn rate 1.5 deg/s, hp 1. Two patrols inside S300 envelope (legs spanning z≈70–130 km),
  one patrol and one fast farther out (z≈180–260 km) — visible on map, out of range, which
  teaches the range ring. States: `AC_ALIVE, AC_FALLING, AC_GONE = range(3)`; FALLING = spiral
  descent (roll into 25 deg bank, pitch down 12 deg, 60% speed) with smoke trail until
  water/terrain impact → splash/explosion event → GONE.
- **Air contact refresh** (ContactBoard): air tracks refresh by range from base:
  (<100 km, 15 s), (<300 km, 30 s), else 60 s. Air contact ids are the aircraft ids
  (prefix `air_` in spawn ids). `estimated_pos` dead-reckons in 3D (altitude included).

## Task S2: S300 definition + SamMissile flight model (TDD, GL-free)

**Files:** Create `sim/sam.py`, `tests/test_sam.py`; modify `sim/arsenal.py` (SamDef dataclass,
S300, S300_TEL, registries).

- `SamDef` frozen dataclass with the locked numbers (name fields analogous to WeaponDef; do
  NOT shoehorn into WeaponDef — different flight regime, separate type is the data-driven way).
- `SamMissile(sam_def, pos_f64, target_aircraft, contact_estimate_fn=None)`: phase ints
  `SPH_EJECT, SPH_BOOST, SPH_MIDCOURSE, SPH_TERMINAL, SPH_DEAD = range(5)`.
  - EJECT 0.6 s: vertical 30 m/s, gravity only.
  - BOOST: ignite; first 1.0 s pure vertical, then steer velocity toward the predicted
    intercept point with a turn-rate limit that scales with speed (≤ max_g*9.81 lateral accel);
    thrust 200 kN until propellant exhausted (mass decreases via mdot).
  - MIDCOURSE (post-burnout coast): each step compute t_go = |r|/max(closing_speed, 50),
    aim = tgt_pos + tgt_vel*t_go (one refinement iteration), steer with G-limit + gravity
    compensation; drag decelerates (cd_from_mach, thin air at altitude).
  - TERMINAL when range < 20 km: full 3D `pn_accel`, G-limit 25.
  - Proximity fuse: every step, if segment prev_pos→pos passes within 25 m of the target
    aircraft position → target killed (`aircraft.kill()` → AC_FALLING), missile dead with
    `impact_pos` at closest approach. Direct hits count too.
  - Self-destruct per locked numbers; ground/water impact kills it.
- Integration: scalar math like the optimized `sim/missile.py` (follow its patterns — the
  perf pass deliberately de-numpyed these hot loops).
- Tests (write first): definition sane; cold launch vertical (no lateral drift in EJECT);
  boost reaches > Mach 4 by burnout and > 8 km alt on a high shot; **e2e intercepts** —
  crossing patrol at 60 km/6500 m killed (closest approach < 25 m, time < 90 s);
  long shot 130 km/6500 m killed; out-of-envelope 200 km → honest miss + self-destruct
  (aircraft survives); maneuvering-ish fast type at 40 km killed; determinism (bit-identical
  reruns); existing 143 tests untouched and green.
- Commit `feat: S-300 SAM flight model with proximity fuse`.
